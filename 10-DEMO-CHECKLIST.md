## Demo Flow
1. Open website
2. Open Origami Lens
3. Click Inspect Page
4. Show scan
5. Show health score
6. Show issues
7. Open an issue
8. Show Problem → Cause → Impact → Suggested Fix

## Key Explanation
"The MVP follows a deterministic-first approach. We collect real webpage evidence and use rules to identify issues. AI then explains the findings and suggests fixes."
